# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - Human Assistance Network

## 1. Executive Summary
The proposed platform is a "Daily Life Assistance Platform" or "Human Assistance Network" designed to provide trusted, on-demand human assistance. It bridges the gap between digital task management and real-world caregiving, offering services that range from elderly support to safe transport and daily errands.

## 2. Product Positioning
Positioned as a trusted companion ecosystem, the platform differentiates itself from standard delivery or task apps by adding a crucial **"human care layer."** It sits at the intersection of services like Urban Company, Porter, Uber, and Practo, catering specifically to individuals living alone, busy professionals, elderly citizens, and NRI families.

## 3. Core Services
* **Care & Companion Services:** Hospital companion, elderly check-ins, walking companion, child pickup/drop assistance, and emotional/social companionship.
* **Daily Task Services:** Grocery shopping, pharmacy pickup, document submission, queue standing, utility bill payments, and parcel delivery.
* **Health & Emergency:** Medicine reminders, SOS alerts, ambulance coordination, live caregiver tracking, and priority emergency booking.
* **Transport Assistance:** Safe cab companion, women's safety ride monitor, late-night tracking, and verified driver assistance.

## 4. Platform Ecosystem & Use Cases
The ecosystem consists of three main components: User App, Helper App, and Admin Portal.

### 4.1 User Side (The Requester)
* **Use Cases:**
  * Book a trusted helper for an elderly parent's hospital visit.
  * Request emergency SOS support.
  * Track helper status and location in real-time.
  * Securely pay for services.
  * Coordinate via **"Family Circle"** (e.g., NRI children booking and monitoring care for parents in India).

### 4.2 Helper Side (The Provider)
* **Use Cases:**
  * View and accept nearby tasks based on skills and availability.
  * Navigate to task locations and log progress step-by-step.
  * Complete multi-tier verification (Aadhaar, face, police, background checks).
  * Track earnings, task history, and reputation score.
  * Access emergency contacts during active tasks.

### 4.3 Admin Side (The Operator)
* **Use Cases:**
  * Monitor safety and detect anomalies (route deviations, task delays).
  * Manage helper onboarding, verification, and skill badge assignment.
  * Handle emergency escalations and dispatch support.
  * Track revenue, platform health, and fraud detection.

## 5. Key Workflows (Flows)

### 5.1 Service Booking Flow
1. **Request:** User selects a category (e.g., Elderly Assistance) and provides task details (time, location, specific instructions).
2. **Matching:** App matches the request with verified helpers nearby based on required skills/badges.
3. **Acceptance:** Helper reviews the task details and accepts the job.
4. **Confirmation:** User confirms the booking and authorizes payment.

### 5.2 Live Task Execution Flow
1. **Initiation:** Helper starts the journey; User and "Family Circle" receive a live tracking link.
2. **Progress Updates:** Helper updates status systematically (e.g., "Reached Supermarket", "3/10 items collected", "Patient reached hospital").
3. **Safety Monitoring:** AI Safety Layer monitors the route and time, triggering alerts if unusual behavior or delays are detected.
4. **Completion:** Task is marked complete; User rates the Helper and leaves a review.

### 5.3 Emergency SOS Flow
1. **Trigger:** User or Helper presses the SOS button in the app.
2. **Activation:** Live audio/video feed starts automatically.
3. **Alert:** Immediate notifications are sent to the "Family Circle" and the Admin Support Team.
4. **Action:** Admin coordinates with local authorities, emergency contacts, or ambulance services.

## 6. Differentiating Features
* **Family Circle:** Allows remote family members to book, pay, and monitor live status (Huge NRI market potential).
* **AI Safety Layer:** Automated detection of task delays, unusual routes, and voice distress to ensure utmost safety.

## 7. Revenue Model
1. **Commission Per Task:** 15–25% fee on each completed booking.
2. **Subscription Plans:** Monthly fee for priority support, dedicated helpers, unlimited tracking, and emergency services.
3. **Corporate Partnerships:** B2B packages for employee assistance and elderly parent support.
4. **Healthcare Partnerships:** Integration with hospitals for patient transport, companion booking, and medicine delivery.

## 8. Launch Strategy
* **Initial Phase:** Focus strictly on **Elderly Assistance + Hospital Companion**.
  * *Why?* High emotional value, less competition, easier trust positioning, recurring customers, and strong word-of-mouth.
* **Expansion Phase:** Gradually introduce Grocery, Companion, Daily Help, Transport, and Medical Errands once trust and a reliable helper base are established.
